About Toram Online :

Toram Online is a highly compatible, 'Stylized' MMORPG (Massively Multiplayer Online Role-Playing Game) with large player base and incredible storyline. The game runs well on both high and low-end devices, without sacrificing its graphics too much, instead focusing on a more low-poly stylized art style unique to itself. As new competition in the MMORPG genre increase, Toram Online and its player base stands strong, gathering new players looking for a “chilled” MMORPG, to older veterans constantly enjoying the game.

Toram allows you to have fun no matter your play style, be it the lone wolf or that one person who can't have fun without their friends. You can play solo or make a party and go on a journey with friends or run into new people along the way. The Toram community in-game is just as helpful as outside the game - in forums - making it easy for newer players. The community while friendly and laid back does have its share of rare idiots trying to ruin everyone's day but Toram does have its own measures and restrictions within the chat. Within the community you can find veterans willing to party up and help lower levels rank up, lower level players looking to have fun and grow with other newer players, and those who just enjoy lounging around, chatting and enjoying the scenery of different areas/maps.

Why choose Toram

Whilst there are many other popular games such as

  • Darksouls
  • Skyrim
  • Final Fantasy series
  • Elder Scrolls Online

Toram Online really makes a difference with its minimal spec requirements while still having nearly as much functionality and area exploration as the more popular titles. The game's initial download size is only a few 100mb with the ingame downloads only being downloaded automatically when required. These consist of map downloads (and considering the number of maps there are it makes sense) as well as other downloads like events, avatars items and equipment/weapons. On the topic of events, toram not only has regular updates, but regular annual events which happen each year -such as the 'XMas Event'. But the Asobimo team didnt stop there... along with the seasonal events there are also shorter "special" events - '5 Million Downloads' - and collaboration events -'ReZero Collaboration 1 & 2'. In these events, you get to take on event quests, fight event specific monsters and get event limited collectibles and equipment. The event quests can be either collect quests, where items can be collected through defeating specific - "normal monsters" - or battle quests where you partake in event specific battles.

Toram's Approach :


Toram approaches character creation dynamically, allowing players to design their own character from various options meanwhile class selection is approached through weapon selection and skills. Initially you are able to choose a weapon class from 5 different weapon classes, but later on throughout the game, the skills and stat points you allocate allow you to truly define or even change your class, thus "dynamic". The initial choice is to give you the corresponding initial skill in the skill tree for that weapon class and is a means of making the game more beginner friendly.
